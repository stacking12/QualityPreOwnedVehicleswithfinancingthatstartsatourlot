About our Buy Here Pay Here Essex County dealership

Buy Here Pay Here Essex County is a used car dealership on Broad Street in downtown Newark that sells and finances its own inventory. That single difference is what makes us useful to drivers who keep getting turned down everywhere else.

At a traditional dealership, the car is chosen on the lot and the loan is decided somewhere else by a bank that has never met you. We removed that step. When you buy from us, the vehicle and the payment plan both come from the same place, and the person reviewing your application is the same person who will hand you the keys.

That means we can look past a credit score to the things that actually predict whether a payment gets made: your income, how long you have worked where you work, and the size of payment that realistically fits your month. Plenty of our customers are rebuilding after a divorce, a medical year, a repossession, or a first job with no credit history at all. None of that disqualifies you here.

Our lot carries sedans, SUVs and work-ready vans priced for real Newark budgets. Every vehicle is inspected before it is offered for sale, and we tell you what we know about it. We would rather sell you a car you can keep than a car you hand back in four months, so the whole process is built around honest numbers and a payment schedule you agree to out loud before anything is signed.

We serve drivers across Essex County, including Newark, East Orange, Irvington, Bloomfield, Belleville and Orange, and we are easy to reach by bus or by car from anywhere downtown.

Frequently asked questions

The questions we hear most often at the desk in Newark.

What does "buy here pay here" actually mean?

It means the dealership finances the car itself instead of sending your application to an outside bank or credit union. You buy the vehicle here and you make your payments here, to us. Because we hold the loan, we decide who gets approved, which is why drivers with damaged or thin credit have a real path to ownership at our Newark lot.

Can I get approved with bad credit or no credit at all?

Yes, in most cases. We do not use your credit score as the deciding factor. What matters is verifiable income, how long you have held your job, a valid driver's license and proof of where you live. Past repossessions, collections and bankruptcies do not automatically disqualify you.

How much do I need for a down payment?

It depends on the vehicle you choose and your income, and we keep the requirement as low as we responsibly can. A larger down payment lowers your weekly or biweekly payment, so we will show you a couple of combinations and let you pick the one that fits your budget. Call 862-256-7005 with the vehicle you are interested in and we can give you a straight number.

What do I need to bring to drive home today?

Bring a valid driver's license, recent pay stubs or other proof of income, a utility bill or lease showing your address, and your down payment. With those in hand, most customers complete the paperwork and leave with the car during the same visit.

How often will I make payments, and where?

Payment schedules are set weekly or biweekly to line up with your payday, and the amount and dates are agreed before you sign. You can pay in person at 972 Broad St #1 in Newark or arrange another method with our office. If something unexpected comes up, call us before the due date and we will do what we can to work with you.
